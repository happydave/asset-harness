## Goal

Add **Z-Anime** (`z-anime-base-bf16`) as a third selectable engine and complete the Turbo vs Base vs
Z-Anime txt2img side-by-side deferred by **WI 924** (see [2026-07-14-zimage-base.md](2026-07-14-zimage-base.md)).
(asset-harness SideQuest WI 932.)

## Tooling

- **Model:** Z-Anime — `z-anime-base-bf16.safetensors`. `SeeSee21/Z-Anime` on HuggingFace: a **full
  fine-tune** (not a LoRA) of **Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image** (Base). Non-distilled → uses the same regime as the
  `base` preset (real cfg, encoded negative, 20 steps / cfg 4.0 / euler / shift 3.0). `--cfg`/`--steps`
  override.
- Same shared CLIP/VAE/AuraFlow-shift as the rest of the Z-Image family; runs as a `zanime` preset in
  both `run_zimage_controlnet.py` and `gen_albedo.py`.

## Inputs

  pastel studio background, front view, crisp" — identical to the WI 924 side-by-side.

## Result

- **Samples:** [`samples-2026-07-14-zanime/`](samples-2026-07-14-zanime/) — `txt2img_turbo.png`,
  `txt2img_base.png`, `txt2img_zanime.png` (identical prompt/seed) + `zanime_last_run.json`.
- **Verdict:** Z-Anime produces the **strongest, most canonical anime look** of the three — cleaner cel
  shading, glossier eyes, a more committed anime aesthetic — where Turbo is photoreal-leaning and Base is
  a softer general-stylized/chibi look. All three outputs are distinct (pixel-verified); turbo and base
  reproduced their exact WI 924 pixel hashes, confirming the new preset did not disturb them.
- **Role:** Z-Anime = the engine for **anime-specific** txt2img; Base stays the general stylized engine;
  Turbo stays default / photoreal / the ControlNet-sprite engine.
