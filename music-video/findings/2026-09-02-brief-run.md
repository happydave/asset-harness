# Findings: first brief-driven run — a video from one JSON file, end to end

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Track:** music-video
**Verdict:** works — `run_brief.py` took the example brief to a rendered 75 s cut and a 56.07 s loop
cut in provisional mode, and a song re-pick re-derived the edit without regenerating a single still

## Goal

The WI 1180 worked example: prove that making a music video is now filling
[`brief.json`](samples-2026-09-02-brief-run/brief.json) and running one command, with every
candidate, score, verdict and pick recorded in the manifest — and commit the run's records (not its
media) as the few-shot material a small-context operator learns from.

## Tooling

- **Base models:** ACE-Step 1.5 XL turbo (`acestep_v1.5_xl_turbo_bf16`), Z-Image base
  (`z_image_bf16`), Wan2.2 i2v-A14B fp8_scaled (high + low noise)
- **LoRA(s):** `wan2.2_i2v_lightx2v_4steps_lora_v1_{high,low}_noise`
- **Workflow / nodes:** the track's `generate_song.py` / `generate_still.py` / `generate_clip.py`
  graphs via `select_song.py`, `cull_stills.py`, `clip_candidates.py`, driven by `run_brief.py`
- **Other software:** Demucs + WhisperX (`align_posthoc.py`, CPU), Audiobox Aesthetics 0.0.4, CLIP
  ViT-B/32 + PickScore v1 (scoring venv, CPU), ffmpeg (render, loop finish, sheets), ComfyUI 0.22.0 on `ai2`

## Inputs

- **Brief:** [`brief.json`](samples-2026-09-02-brief-run/brief.json) — the Clamor song spec, the WI
  1004 style string, 8 shots × 2 lines (7 stills, 1 hero clip animating shot 4's still), the WI
  1159 loop block, `song_seeds 701,702,703`, `stills_n 3`, `clips_n 2`.
- **Key params:** turbo 8 steps / cfg 1; Z-Image base 20 / 4.0 at 1280×720; Wan recipe (4 steps,
  cfg 1, shift 5, 33 f); alignment `small.en`, int8, CPU.

## Steps

1. `python3 run_brief.py run inputs/clamor_hold_the_line.brief.json --out outputs/runs/clamor_hold_the_line --mode provisional`
2. The first run stopped at render: the loop finish refused its cut by two frames (fixed below), and
   the timeline it had derived on the auto-picked take was bad (8/16 lines interpolated). With the new
   low-confidence stop in place, `resume` refused to render on that timeline.
3. `python3 repick.py pick <manifest> --song --to song/clamor_hold_the_line_seed701.flac --by owner --reason "..."`
4. `python3 run_brief.py resume … --mode provisional` — re-derived the timeline for seed 701, carried
   every shot over (`shots reset []`), re-rendered the cut and the loop in 61 s.

## Result

- **Sample output(s):** [`samples-2026-09-02-brief-run/`](samples-2026-09-02-brief-run/) —
  manifest (song block with 3 candidates and the owner re-pick history; 7 still candidate sets of 3
  with CLIP/PickScore ratios; 1 clip candidate set of 2 with integrity + motion), `run.json`, both
  pick forms and tile keys, the timeline, the loop report, and a four-frame strip of the cut
  ([5 s, 30 s, 58 s, 70 s](samples-2026-09-02-brief-run/cut_frames_5s_30s_58s_70s.png)).
- **Timings (first run, 17 m 40 s total):** preflight 1 s · song sweep + Audiobox ≈ 1.5 min
  (seed 703 truncated → culled; seed 702 picked at CE 7.31) · alignment 27 s · 21 stills ≈ 10.5 min ·
  2 clips ≈ 4.5 min · render ≈ 1 min. **Resume after the song re-pick: 61 s** (alignment 27 s +
  render), zero regeneration.
- **The cut:** 75.0 s, 5.3 MB; loop cut 56.07 s (1682 frames), gates pass — video gap 0.0415 vs the
  keyframe norm, audio join 0.15× local, **codec padding 18.67 ms** (the searched length is off the
  AAC grid; `--webm` remains the escape, per WI 1159).
- **What worked:** every stage's record landed in one manifest; the re-pick path did exactly what the
  design promised (timeline and partition re-derived, stills kept because the lyric text was
  unchanged, only the render repeated).
- **What failed and was fixed in this run:** (1) `loop_finish.wrap` cut by seconds from a 4-decimal
  length, admitting two extra frames on a searched length that was not on the frame grid — now works
  in whole frames; (2) the driver rendered on a timeline with half its lines interpolated — it now
  stops when more than a quarter are, offering tap or re-pick; (3) the resume checked the stale
  timeline before noticing the song re-pick — re-ordered.
- **Open:** the aligner's weakness on the seed 702 take is filed as a follow-up (structure-aware
  reconcile for a repeated chorus); the owner's watch of the cut and any re-picks.

## Repeatability

- Seeds are recorded per candidate; the same brief and seeds regenerate the same candidate set. The
  edit is a pure function of the manifest, so any re-pick is a re-render. The timeline depends on the
  ASR pass and varied between generations of the same seed (16/16 in WI 1001, 12/16 today).
