# The Lantern Fleet — "Between the Lights" (2026-07-29)

A third [Lantern Fleet](../lantern-fleet/lore.md) song, written to develop the universe (owner: "we'll
hang onto this and fill it out more"). Where session 1 gave the ride ("Lamplighter") and the elegy ("The
Island That Went Dark"), this is the **crossing itself** — the long solo sail across the empty gulf
between two islands. The whole song is the journey, which is why it's scored to a **continuous chained
skiff-flight** (two 6 s Wan clips, clip 2 from clip 1's cleaned last frame — the video's spine).

Written against the [refinement checklist](../clamor-night/lyrics.md).

---

## "Between the Lights" (the long crossing)

POV: a lamplighter mid-crossing, no island under the keel, between the light just left and the light not
yet reached. Contemplative, warm, a slow lift toward arrival.

```
[verse]
No island under me now, just the deep and the drift
A striker at my collar and a sail full of lift
Behind me a light I lit, ahead one gone to grey
And a whole silver nothing in between, and a day to cross the way

[chorus]
I'm between the lights, between the lights tonight
Just me and the little flame and the falling amber light
Nobody to steady, nobody to save
Just the long slow crossing and the small warm brave

[verse]
The cloud sea breathes below like it's asking me to land
But you don't touch the Underdark, you keep a steady hand
So I sing to keep me company, I sing to keep it slow
The oldest lamplighter song, the only one I know

[chorus]
I'm between the lights, between the lights tonight
Just me and the little flame and the falling amber light
Nobody to steady, nobody to save
Just the long slow crossing and the small warm brave

[bridge]
And the far Beacon rises like a coal in the dark
And I lean on the tiller and I aim for the spark
Every crossing ends in a light if you hold the line
And the next one's always mine
```

**ACE tags:** `warm contemplative folk, gentle solo female vocal, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft
building strings, spacious and airy, hopeful melancholy, cinematic, major key, slow build` — **80 bpm,
D major, 75 s.**
