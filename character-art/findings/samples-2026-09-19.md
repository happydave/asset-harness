# Findings: Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 holds non-human race features

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Track:** character-art
**Verdict:** works

## Goal

Settle the risk that gated the track's architecture: WI 1591 round 4 eliminated every no-training
identity method (InstantID, PuLID, IP-Adapter FaceID, ReActor) for non-human characters, because
all of them sit on an ArcFace-family **human** face embedding. That left the edit model as the only
re-projection mechanism, and nobody had published whether it holds horns, snouts and tusks.

## Tooling

- **Base model:** Qwen-Image-Edit-2511, `qwen_image_edit_2511_int8_convrot.safetensors`
- **Text encoder / VAE:** `qwen_2.5_vl_7b_fp8_scaled`, `qwen_image_vae`
- **LoRA:** `tarn59/character_turnaround_sheet_qwen_edit_2511` v3 (turnaround sheets only; the
  re-pose runs use no LoRA)
- **Source portraits:** `waiIllustriousSDXL_v170`, CLIP skip −2, dpmpp_2m/karras, 30 steps, CFG 5.0
- **Workflow:** `../prototypes/qwen-edit-2511/run_arm_a.py`, traced from Comfy-Org's
  `image_qwen_image_edit_2511_int8.json`. ComfyUI v0.34.6 in WI 1600's digest-pinned ROCm container
  on `ai2` (R9700, gfx1201, ROCm 7.2).

## Result

**Nothing was humanised in any of 11 runs**, across three non-human races, two mechanisms
(turnaround sheet and direct re-pose) and four view types. No horn became a nub, no snout became a
nose, no tusk became a tooth.

| Subject | Features | In a 5-panel sheet | At full per-view resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiefling | horns / tail / skin hue | intact | intact |
| Half-orc | tusks / jaw / skin hue | intact | intact |
| Dragonborn | snout / scale texture | *softened* | **intact** |
| Dragonborn | no human nose or lip line | intact | intact |
| Human (control) | identity / hair / no spurious features | intact | intact |

The dragonborn's softening in the sheet is a **resolution budget** effect, not a model one: a
2600 px five-view sheet gives each view 520 px against the source's 600. At 600×1080 the same model
and quantisation render the snout crisp with individual teeth — see
`samples-2026-09-19/profile_heads_fullres_vs_source.png`.

Feature survival tracked **discreteness and silhouette contribution**, not how non-human the
feature was. Horns, tails and tusks were perfect everywhere; only surface texture ever softened,
and only when starved of pixels.

## Cost

~500 s per 2600×1080 five-view sheet, ~160 s per 600×1080 single view, on one R9700.
Peak VRAM **32,509 MiB of 32,624**.
